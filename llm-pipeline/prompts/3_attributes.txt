===== MORPHOSYNTAX =====
TASK (Event › General Event: MORPHOSYNTACTIC information). For every EVENT marked ⟦ID text⟧ in the
sentence give its morphosyntactic attributes.

pos: VERB | NOUN | ADJECTIVE | PREPOSITION (prepositional / lexicalised expressions: "em remissão completa",
     "em estado grave") | PRONOUN (e.g. relative "que").
polarity: NEG if the event is negated ("não", "sem", "nem", "nada", "ausência de", negative verbs such as
     "deixar de"); otherwise POS. "Doente sem antecedentes relevantes" → antecedentes relevantes = NEG.
tense (of the compound tense): PRESENT | PAST | FUTURE | NONE. Verbs AND predicative complements of a copula get
     a tense, taken from the copula ("O FLT3 foi positivo" → positivo: ADJECTIVE, PAST, PERFECTIVE); other
     non-verbal events get NONE.
     NON-FINITE FORMS HAVE NO TENSE → tense NONE:
       - every GERUND ("-ndo"): "tratando-se", "condicionando", "estendendo-se", "associando-se", "sendo" → NONE
         (a gerund is never PAST, even in a past narrative);
       - a free infinitive: "por apresentar", "para realizar", "decide-se propor" (propor) → NONE;
       - a participle used as an adjective: "leucemia associada a mutação" (associada) → NONE.
     A present-tense form used to narrate the past keeps tense PRESENT ("Em março de 2020, inicia
     quimioterapia" → inicia: PRESENT, vform NONE). A conjugated verb (inicia, realizou, apresentava) is FINITE:
     vform NONE; PARTICIPLE only for -ado / -ido / -ada / -ida forms.
     A participle or infinitive INSIDE a compound or periphrastic verb takes the tense of its auxiliary:
       "foi submetido" → submetido PAST; "tinha iniciado" → iniciado PAST; "esteve a fazer" → fazer PAST.
aspect: PROGRESSIVE ("está a ser acompanhado") | PERFECTIVE ("fez", "mostrou") | IMPERFECTIVE ("encontrava-se",
     "apresentava") | PERFECTIVE_PROGRESSIVE ("esteve a fazer") | IMPERFECTIVE_PROGRESSIVE ("estava a ser
     seguido") | NONE. Same rule as tense for non-verbal events.
vform (verbs only, else NONE): GERUNDIVE ("tratando-se") | INFINITIVE ("apresentar") | PARTICIPLE ("associada") |
     NONE (finite verb).
mood (verbs only, else NONE): SUBJUNCTIVE ("que faça") | CONDITIONAL ("aceitaria", "faria") | FUTURE ("estará") |
     NONE (indicative).
modality (verbs only, else NONE): DEVER | PODER | TER_DE | SER_CAPAZ_DE when the event is governed by that modal
     verb ("deve cumprir" → cumprir: DEVER), otherwise NONE.

===== SEMANTICS =====
TASK (Event › General Event: SEMANTIC information). For every VERB event listed below give its
aspectual CLASS and its TYPE (Med2Story manual, General Event attributes 1 and 2). The events are marked ⟦ID text⟧
in the sentence.

CLASS – go down this list and take the FIRST that fits:
  1. REPORTING – the subject declares, narrates or informs: referir, relatar, declarar, dizer, explicar, informar,
     mencionar ("É de referir que…" → referir; "O paciente relatou astenia" → relatou).
  2. PERCEPTION – a PERSON perceives with the senses: ver, ouvir ("O doente ouviu a explicação" → ouviu).
     An examination that "revela / mostra" a finding is NOT perception (→ STATE).
  3. ASPECTUAL – the verb marks the BEGINNING, CONTINUATION or END of ANOTHER event: começar, iniciar, continuar,
     manter, terminar, concluir, interromper ("O doente começou a apresentar sintomas" → começou;
     "inicia queixas de dor" → inicia; "terminou o tratamento" → terminou).
  4. I_ACTION – an action that introduces ANOTHER event as its argument: tentar, adiar, evitar, cancelar, pedir,
     solicitar, ordenar, autorizar, prometer, oferecer, propor, decidir, sugerir, concordar
     ("Decide-se propor o doente para tratamento" → Decide-se = I_ACTION, propor = I_ACTION; "Solicita-se
     criopreservação" → Solicita-se = I_ACTION; "Propõe-se tratamento" → Propõe-se = I_ACTION).
  5. I_STATE – a mental state about another event: acreditar, pensar, imaginar, querer, esperar, recear,
     preocupar-se ("O paciente acredita que a dor…" → acredita).
  6. STATE – something HOLDS true; having, showing or being in a condition: apresentar ("apresentava anemia"),
     mostrar / revelar / evidenciar when an exam shows a finding ("O hemograma mostrou anemia" → mostrou = STATE),
     encontrar-se, manter-se em, ter, estar.
  7. OCCURRENCE – any other situation that HAPPENS: realizar, fazer, internar, transferir, confirmar, constatar,
     identificar, detetar, diagnosticar, falecer, receber, instalar-se, condicionar, estender-se
     (confirmar a diagnosis is OCCURRENCE, not REPORTING)
     ("Em fevereiro, realizou exames" → realizou).

TYPE – ask "what kind of situation is it?":
  STATE – the situation simply HOLDS; nothing changes (apresentava, mostrou, se encontrava em tratamento, tem).
     Verbs of class STATE are almost always type STATE.
  TRANSITION – something CHANGES or is COMPLETED: the situation has an END POINT and leaves a new state (a
     result). Includes achievements, which happen at one moment (iniciar, detetar, identificar, confirmar,
     falecer, transferir, internar, instalar-se, decidir) and accomplishments, which take time but reach an
     end (realizar um exame, fazer quimioterapia, propor, receber tratamento). In clinical reports most action
     verbs are TRANSITION ("Decide-se propor o doente para tratamento" → Decide-se and propor = TRANSITION).
  PROCESS – a dynamic activity with NO natural end point, described only as going on for some time
     ("O doente dormiu bem durante a primeira noite" → dormiu; "está a ser acompanhado"; "tem sido seguido";
     "foi piorando progressivamente"). Use PROCESS ONLY for such ongoing activities without a result.
  Quick test: done / started / ended / changed? → TRANSITION. Just holds? → STATE. Goes on with no end? → PROCESS.

===== TIMEX_VALUE =====
TASK (Temporal Expressions › value and function). Normalise every temporal expression marked ⟦T# text⟧
(TIDES 2005 / ISO-TimeML). The report was written on <<dct>> (DOCTIME); resolve underspecified and relative
expressions against it.

value, according to the type:
  DATE: "2019-01-25", "2018-12", "2019"; a bare month in a report written on 2019-05-15 → "janeiro" = "2019-01";
        "recentemente"/"recente" → "PAST_REF"; "atualmente"/"neste momento" → "PRESENT_REF"; vague future →
        "FUTURE_REF".
  TIME: "2019-01-25T16:00" or "T16:00" ("4 p.m." → "T16:00").
  DURATION: "P2M" (dois meses), "P10M", "P3D", "PT2H", "P79Y" (79 anos de idade).
  SET: the period, with freq or quant: "duas vezes por semana" → "P1W", freq "2X"; "de dois em dois dias" → "P2D",
        quant "EVERY"; "três dias por semana" → "P1W", quant "EACH"; "diariamente" → "P1D", quant "EVERY".
freq / quant: only for SET, otherwise "".
function: ADMISSION_TIME (date the patient was admitted) | DISCHARGE_TIME (date the patient was discharged) | NONE.
anchor: if the expression is anaphoric or relative to ANOTHER temporal expression of the list ("nesse dia", "no dia
        seguinte", "dois dias depois"), give that expression's ID (e.g. "T3"); otherwise "".
